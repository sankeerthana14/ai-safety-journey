
# AI Safety Research & Learning

Hi! I'm Sankeerthana, a PhD researcher at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore.

My research focuses on the reliability and safety of language models, particularly understanding how internal representations relate to model behaviour and how safety-relevant signals can be detected and used to guide interventions.

I'm especially interested in model introspection, mechanistic interpretability, and understanding how training shapes model behaviour and alignment.

This repository brings together my research projects, independent explorations, and learning resources as I develop my understanding of AI safety.

## Selected Research Projects

### 1. BRIDGE — Evidence Insufficiency in Medical LLMs

Investigating whether evidence insufficiency can be detected from internal model representations and whether detection-guided interventions can reduce unsupported responses in medical LLMs.

**Methods:** Hidden-state analysis, linear probing, activation steering, behavioural evaluation.

[View BRIDGE Repository](https://github.com/sankeerthana14/BRIDGE)

### 2. Probing Jailbreak Brittleness — Capability Limits vs. Alignment Failures

An independent AI safety investigation developed through the Apart Research Global South AI Safety Research Sprint (June 2026).

This project explores jailbreak susceptibility across language models, investigating the distinction between failures to recognise unsafe requests and failures to respond appropriately despite recognising risk.

**Methods:** Layer-wise activation extraction, linear probing, jailbreak evaluation, representation analysis.

[View Jailbreak Brittleness Repository](https://github.com/sankeerthana14/jailbreak-brittleness)
